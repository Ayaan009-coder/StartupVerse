# 🚀 StartupVerse

> Your startup journey starts here.

StartupVerse is a React-based educational platform designed for aspiring founders and students who want to understand the world of startups.

The goal of StartupVerse is simple:

**If someone gets a startup idea, they should be able to come to StartupVerse and find answers to their fundamental startup questions in one place.**

---

## 📌 About The Project

Starting a startup can be confusing.

There are hundreds of terms, concepts, skills, business models and decisions that a first-time founder needs to understand.

StartupVerse brings these concepts together in a simple and beginner-friendly platform.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🗺️ Startup Roadmap

A step-by-step roadmap covering:

- Finding a problem
- Understanding the market
- Validating an idea
- Building an MVP
- Getting first customers
- Product-Market Fit
- Building the business
- Scaling

---

### 🧠 Founder Skills

Learn about the important skills required by founders:

- Problem Solving
- Sales
- Marketing
- Product Management
- Finance
- Technology
- Leadership
- Data & Analytics

---

### 📖 Startup Dictionary

Understand common startup terminology in simple language.

Some examples:

- B2B
- B2C
- D2C
- MVP
- PMF
- CAC
- LTV
- TAM
- MRR
- ARR
- Burn Rate
- Runway
- GTM

---

### 🏢 Startup Stories

Explore stories and case studies of successful startups such as:

- Zomato
- Swiggy
- Zepto
- Razorpay
- Meesho
- CRED

The section focuses on understanding:

- How the startup started
- The problem it solved
- Its business model
- Growth journey
- Important decisions
- Lessons for aspiring founders

---

### 🎙️ Founder Podcasts

A dedicated section to discover founder interviews and podcasts where entrepreneurs discuss:

- Their startup journey
- Failures
- Challenges
- Business decisions
- Product
- Growth
- Fundraising
- Entrepreneurship
